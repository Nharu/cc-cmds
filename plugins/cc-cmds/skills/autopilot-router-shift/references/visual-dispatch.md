#### Dispatching the visual stage

When `steps[]` carries exactly one step whose `skill` is `visual`, and every step its `depends_on` names — the audit, or the design when the plan has no audit — ended `정상 완료`, dispatch it — `--segment -`, stage kind `visual`:

```
gate.sh act --manifest <매니페스트> --kind skill --target <home alias> --segment - \
  --cutpoint 커밋 --surface 워크트리쓰기 --snapshot-digest <H> --emit-digest \
  -- visual -p "/cc-cmds:visual-unattended <스냅숏 design_doc>"
```

Wait on it as *Waiting on a stage* describes. The stage is complete when its artifact predicate holds: the literal `시각 디자인을 검토 통과로 확정했습니다.` in its stream, and the document's visual sidecar reading `**상태**: 검토 통과` or `**상태**: 발행됨` (a re-kicked run reuses its source's sidecar without generating it again, rewriting only its record path line to this run's). Either alone is a `공허한 성공`, which 「Continuing a stage that ended its turn in prose」 takes. **A stage that made a new design system completes another way**: the literal `디자인 시스템을 사람 확인으로 넘겼습니다.` in its stream and the design system waiting for a person (`visual-check.sh confirm <설계 문서>` prints a line beginning `확인 대기 — 문서 ` or exactly `확인 형식 오류`); its row carries `시각 종단=디자인 시스템`, and no screen exists yet. The visual step is not a segment and its rows carry `세그먼트=-`. **A visual step that did not end `정상 완료` — a halt included — holds only the screen slices**: plan and dispatch every slice whose `**화면**` field reads `없음` as if the step had not been there.

**The publish step.** When `steps[]` carries exactly one step whose `skill` is `visual-publish` (`S2P`), and the step its `depends_on` names (`S2V`) ended `정상 완료`, dispatch it — `--segment -`, stage kind `visual-publish`:

```
gate.sh act --manifest <매니페스트> --kind skill --target <home alias> --segment - \
  --cutpoint 커밋 --surface 워크트리쓰기 --snapshot-digest <H> --emit-digest \
  -- visual-publish -p "/cc-cmds:visual-publish-unattended <스냅숏 design_doc>"
```

It is complete when the literal `시각 발행 단계를 마쳤습니다.` is in its stream and either the document's visual sidecar reads `**상태**: 발행됨`, a person's `거부` or `발행하지 않음` answer to its record stands, or its design system phase finished — system.md's `**확인**` line is a person's approval naming this document, backed by a person's `승인` to the run's `디자인-시스템-판정` record that line names, and the sidecar is absent or pins another palette; either half alone is a `공허한 성공`. **When the pinned publish account is blocked by its weekly window, the router migrates the step to an unattended account with room and the gate records that migration**; the stage then publishes the canvases again under the new account and shares them, so a re-dispatched `S2P` may carry new canvas URLs, and nothing here chooses or records the account. **A `pinned-publish-no-successor` park** is the router finding no account to migrate to: like any other park of the step, write its recovery line — the original account's reset time and, per candidate, why it could not take the step — in `막힌 지점`, and dispatch `S2P` again as above once the original account has reset (item 2's 「Any other park of the step」 covers it). **An `S2P` that did not end `정상 완료` — a halt included — holds only the screen slices**, as the visual step's does, and no implement step waits on `S2P`. Its `시각-판정`, `금지-후보`, `발행-실패` and `디자인-시스템-판정` records take a person's answer only; the carry act hands it on like any answered halt.

**Dispatching the two steps again in the same run.** A step whose last row ended `정상 완료` is dispatched again only on the design system round trip, and the gate refuses every other re-dispatch with a reason line:

- After `S2V` ended on the design system (`시각 종단=디자인 시스템`), dispatch `S2P`: it publishes the design system and halts at `디자인-시스템-판정`.
- After `S2P` ended `정상 완료` in its design system phase, dispatch `S2V` again — the gate takes it only when `S2V`'s last row carries `시각 종단=디자인 시스템`, the confirmation line is exactly `사람 승인 — 문서 <이 문서 키>`, its `런` is this run's id, its `사람 답` names this run's latest `디자인-시스템-판정` record, a person's `승인` answer row stands for that record and no refusal does, the line's `tokens.css sha256` equals that record's `제시 tokens.css=` piece, and the sidecar is absent or its design system digest differs from the current one. A hand-written approval line names no such record, so it re-dispatches nothing.

A re-dispatch is a new attempt of the same step: its stage id carries the next attempt number (`S2V#2` after `S2V#1`), and the halt records of the earlier attempts stay where they are.
- After `S2V` ended `정상 완료` on the screens, dispatch `S2P` again — the gate takes it only when `S2V`'s last `정상 완료` row is later in the ledger than `S2P`'s last row.

**Which slices wait.** A slice of `## 구현 슬라이싱` is a screen slice when its `**화면**` field lists screen ids, or when it carries no `**화면**` field and the plan has a visual step or the document has a visual sidecar — a slice that does not say waits for every screen. A slice whose `**화면**` reads `없음` never waits. **The predicate** is `visual-check.sh approval <설계 문서>`, read through the gate as `gate.sh exec … --surface 읽기 -- <plugin root>/orchestrator/visual-check.sh approval <스냅숏 design_doc>` — the helper's absolute path is argv0 and nothing stands before it, because the gate grades its own helper by subcommand only when called that way, and behind `bash` the read is refused whenever the auto-resolve switch is off; exit 0 is a predicate that holds, anything else one that does not. Read `visual-check.sh confirm <스냅숏 design_doc>` beside it the same way: a line beginning `확인 대기 — 문서 ` or reading exactly `확인 형식 오류` is a design system waiting for a person, and while it stands the predicate does not hold whatever `approval` printed — the gate reads the approval as void for the same reason (`디자인 시스템 확인 대기`). When a screen slice's turn comes and the predicate already holds, plan and dispatch it as any slice, with no judgment — unless a person closed its `시각-승인` as `거부` or `무효` in this run, which item 5 settles.

**A predicate held for the design document.** When the predicate's first line is `승인 보류 — 설계 문서 반영 대기` and no re-convergence of that approval's `시각-판정` record has been dispatched yet — the record the sidecar's `**구현 승인**` line names in its ` — 사람 답 <record>` tail, as the absolute path under this run's `halt/` — dispatch the reflection once, on the visual publish step's key:

```
gate.sh act --manifest <매니페스트> --kind skill --target <home alias> --segment - \
  --cutpoint <token> --surface 워크트리쓰기 --snapshot-digest <H> --emit-digest \
  --rationale '중단 답 줄 <line>; 승인 화면 반영' \
  -- reconverge -p "/cc-cmds:design-reconverge <스냅숏 design_doc> 승인 화면 반영 <시각-판정 기록 절대 경로>"
```

Leave the screen slices in their visual-wait park meanwhile and keep dispatching the work outside them. While the reflection's stop record (`**스텝**: Step 3 승인 화면 반영 — 충돌`) stands, the seat reports it as a question for a person, with its one option `결정 첨부 후 다시 반영`, and the screen slices stay held; an answer to it is carried by 「Handing on a person's answer to a halt」. After `완료` the predicate reads `승인 유효` again, and the items below dispatch the screen slices as before. **The reflection's end is not a re-plan signal.** A re-convergence whose scope is `승인 화면 반영` returns no segment to planning, whatever its verdict: even on `판정: 재설계 필요` the plans of the screen-less segments stand, and the next shift reads the predicate again and, on `승인 유효`, plans and dispatches the screen slices by the visual-wait handling below.

**Carrying an answer to the reflection's stop.** That record — `skill` `design-reconverge`, kind `reconverge`, step `Step 3 승인 화면 반영 — 충돌`, the one option `결정 첨부 후 다시 반영` — is a re-convergence stop at its Step 3 in the carry table of 「Handing on a person's answer to a halt」: the answer, with its attachment, is carried to `/cc-cmds:design-reconverge <문서> 승인 화면 반영 <시각-판정 기록 절대 경로>` on the visual publish step's key (`--segment -`), and the fresh session reads it as an answer card. An answer of this run goes the same way: its route is `재부착`, the carry (`--carry-answer`) comes first, and the gate admits the fresh dispatch as a reflection a person's answer opened. `--resume` is not this answer's way; it only re-attaches a reflection session that was cut off. An answer brought over from an earlier run enters by the entry predicate's carried-answer branch. A reflection stop no person answered is not carried — it stays a record waiting for a person — and no new route id stands for it.

**The fixed judgment.** The judgment that waits is one per screen segment, always in the same words, because its id is a hash of the run, the segment and the question:

```
gate.sh act --manifest <매니페스트> --kind judgment --target <home alias> --segment <화면 세그먼트> \
  --cutpoint 커밋 --surface 읽기 --snapshot-digest <H> --emit-digest \
  -- 등급=2 '판단 부류=시각-승인' '기준=시각 승인 대기' '근거=<화면 세그먼트> 의 화면이 고정 좌석에서 구현 승인되었는가'
```

Its `막는 세그먼트` is the screen segment, never the visual step id. Resubmitting it never reaches the two-questions-per-segment cap, which counts distinct ids. Once a `자율 승인` row carries its id in `해소 승인`, do not submit it again: a resubmission after the adoption is refused as spent. **Exit 0 on a resubmission is not an adoption by itself**: the approval is adopted only when that row carries `결정=채택`. A row carrying `결정=거절 소비` is a person's `거부` or `무효` spent, and it leads to item 5, never to item 3.

1. **A screen slice's turn with no predicate.** For that slice and for every planned slice whose `선행` reaches it, write a `segment` `park` row with the plan row's `선행` and a `스코프=cone` `blocked` row anchored on it with `원인=막힘` and `'사유=시각 승인 대기'` — the pair this section calls a visual-wait park — before any branch or worktree is cut for them, and then submit the fixed judgment on the screen segment — one per screen segment when several are waiting. A slice not planned yet gets no row: the planning rule keeps it back while its `선행` is parked. When the predicate already holds, the gate closes the approval as `승인` inside that act and adopts it, so go to item 3. When it does not, the act ends on exit 5; under the **CFI-S4 exception** that holds the screen segment and its `선행` followers and you keep dispatching the work outside them, naming the waiting slices in `막힌 지점`.
2. **Nothing left outside the held set.** End with `사유=승인`. The seat asks the person, naming `/cc-cmds:visual <설계 문서>` to run on the pinned seat and telling them to answer with the label `승인` once they have approved there; an answer in free words does not close the question, so the seat asks again for the label. On each turn read `answered_judgments[]` and the predicate: when `answered_judgments[]` carries that segment's `시각-승인` as `거부` or `무효`, go to item 5 whatever the predicate says; otherwise, when the person's `승인` stands or the predicate holds, submit the same fixed judgment again — it closes and adopts the approval. While a screen slice waits, do not propose done: the open `시각-승인` counts against termination condition 2. **In a run whose plan carries `S2P`**, the seat asks the `S2P` stage's latest `시각-판정` halt record instead of naming `/cc-cmds:visual` — or, while the design system waits for a person, its latest `디자인-시스템-판정` record, since the screens wait on that answer first: a person's `승인` re-attaches the stage, which writes the approval line with the ` — 사람 답 <record>` tail, and then the same fixed judgment submitted again closes and adopts the approval. When the `S2P` stage's latest record is a `전제-불일치` record, or the step was parked where this run cannot recover it — routing is off, or the run's deadline has passed — there is no `시각-판정` record to ask: name that record's `**관측 상세**` — or the `park` row's router verdict and recovery line — in `막힌 지점`, and the seat asks the `시각-승인` question on its own, where only a person's `거부` or `무효` moves the slice, to item 5. Any other park of the step is not a closing: name its recovery line in `막힌 지점`, dispatch `S2P` again as the publish step above says once the cause that line names is fixed, and the seat asks the `시각-판정` record the re-dispatched stage writes — never `거부` or `무효` on the `시각-승인` meanwhile, which would refuse the slice for the rest of the run. **When the approval line already stands and the predicate holds it back**, the person has nothing to answer at the `시각-판정` record: on `승인 보류 — 설계 문서 반영 대기` the seat says the reflection is under way and to wait — or, in a run whose plan carries no `S2P` step, where nothing here dispatches the reflection, to run `/cc-cmds:visual <설계 문서> 구현 승인` again, as the morning report's `대기 — 반영 미파견` line says; on `승인 보류 — 설계 문서 반영 실패 — <사유>` it asks the person to answer the reflection's stop record.
3. **Adopted and the predicate holds.** Adopted means a `자율 승인` row with `결정=채택` carries the id in `해소 승인`. On a segment this run's shift left in a visual-wait park, write `상태=실행중` with the plan row's `선행` and dispatch its implementation as any planned slice. A `park` the fixed-graph driver wrote is not taken over: that run has its end row, the gate refuses its dispatches, and a new run plans the screen slices afresh.
4. **Adopted and the predicate is gone.** A sync or a re-publish removed the `**구현 승인**` line after the adoption. Do not submit the judgment again; leave the slice at `park` and write in `막힌 지점` — and the morning report — 「고정 좌석에서 `구현 승인` 을 다시 쓸 것」 — in a run whose plan carries `S2P`, 「`수정 요청` 뒤 다시 쓰인 `시각-판정` 에 `승인` 으로 답할 것」 instead. When the line stands and the predicate reads `승인 보류`, write instead what item 2 says for it — wait for the reflection on `대기` (or run `/cc-cmds:visual <설계 문서> 구현 승인` again where no `S2P` step dispatches it), answer the reflection's stop record on `실패`. When the predicate holds again, item 3 dispatches it under the approval already closed.
5. **Refused.** A person closed the `시각-승인` as `거부` or `무효`: the screens are not to be built. The segment is already `park`, so submit the same fixed judgment again to consume the refusal (`결정=거절 소비`), and settle the clauses that need those screens as `불가능` citing that answer. A person's `거부` to an `S2P` `시각-판정` or `디자인-시스템-판정` record, or `발행하지 않음` to its `발행-실패` record, is such a closing: the gate writes it inside the `중단 답` act (`처분 사유=중단 답 연동`), and the same resubmission consumes it. Until it is consumed the segment stays held. **A consumed refusal does not make the segment `실행중` again**: its exit 0 is not an adoption, the segment stays `park` for the rest of the run even when the predicate holds, and the gate refuses an implementation dispatch to it.

The same three walls stand around these items: the open `시각-승인` that refuses a dispatch to its segment, the `park` row, and the gate's own refusal of an implementation dispatch carrying a screen slice while the predicate does not hold or after a person closed its `시각-승인` as `거부` or `무효` — which is what stops item 4's or item 5's slice if it were dispatched anyway.
